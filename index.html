Get the app

Put the room on your phone's Home Screen. It opens full screen like any other app, and it can send you a notification when someone @mentions you.

check_circle You're using the app. Turn on Push @mentions to this device in Settings to get notifications.

phone_iphone iPhone and iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari (Chrome and Edge work too on iOS 16.4 or newer).
  2. Tap Share ios_share (on newer iPhones it's in the ••• menu next to the address).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen add_box.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  5. Open Indie Disco from your Home Screen and sign in. Notifications only work from the Home Screen app on iPhone and iPad.
android Android
  1. Chrome: tap the menu more_vert and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen, then Install).
  2. Samsung Internet: tap the menu menu, then Add page to → Home screen.
  3. Firefox: tap the menu more_vert, then Install.
  4. Open Indie Disco from your home screen or app drawer.
computer Computer
  1. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon install_desktop at the right of the address bar (or menu → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app).
  2. Safari on a Mac: File → Add to Dock.

Then, in the app: Settings → Push @mentions to this device.

Play on a speaker or TV

The room plays on this device, so send this device's sound to your speaker. You can still Fire, chat and DJ from here as usual.

cast Google Cast: Chromecast, Nest speakers, Google TV
  1. Open the room in Chrome or Edge on a computer on the same Wi-Fi as the speaker.
  2. Chrome: menu more_vert → Cast, save, and share → Cast…
    Edge: menu more_horiz → More tools → Cast media to device
  3. If it shows Sources, choose Cast tab.
  4. Pick your speaker, TV or speaker group. The room now plays there.
  5. To stop, click cast in the toolbar, then Stop casting.
airplay AirPlay: HomePod, Sonos, Apple TV
  1. iPhone or iPad: open Control Center (swipe down from the top-right corner), tap airplay on the music card, then pick your speaker.
  2. Mac: click Control Center in the menu bar, then the airplay next to Sound, and pick your speaker.
  3. Keep the room open. On iPhone, if the sound stops when the screen locks, keep the room on screen.
android Android: Nest speakers, Chromecast, TVs
  1. Open the Google Home app on the same Wi-Fi as the speaker.
  2. Tap the speaker or speaker group, then Cast my audio.
  3. Come back to the room. Its sound now plays on the speaker.
  4. To a TV: swipe down for Quick Settings and tap Screen Cast (Smart View on Samsung).
bluetooth Bluetooth: any speaker, Amazon Echo, soundbars
  1. Put the speaker in pairing mode. On an Amazon Echo, say “Alexa, pair Bluetooth.”
  2. Pair it in this device's Bluetooth settings (Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices; Mac: System Settings → Bluetooth; phones: Settings → Bluetooth).
  3. The room plays through it like any other sound.

Set the volume on the speaker, and keep the room's own volume up. Closing the room stops the sound.

      The bot

      !plays, !firstplay
      How often the song has played here, and who played it first and last
      !bpm
      The song's tempo; the DJ can set it: !bpm 128
      !suggest theme
      Start a theme vote
      !removeafter, !stay
      Step down after your next song, or stay on after all (the 🚏 buttons have a menu for all of these)
      !removeafter 3
      Step down after 3 songs, counting the one you're playing
      !removeafter 45m, !leavein 45
      Step down in 45 minutes (or 1h30), finishing your song if you're playing
      !bus
      Who's catching the bus, and when
      !autodj
      What the autopilot DJ is doing

      Mods

      !remove name
      Take someone off the decks or the line
      !removeafter 3 name, !leavein 30 name, !stay name
      Put someone of lower rank on the bus, or take them off it
      !fixtags artist - title
      Fix the playing song's tags
      !flagplay id reason
      Flag a play from Recent: broken, quality or offtheme
      !upbot, !downbot, !autodj auto
      The bot DJs, stays off, or decides for itself again
      !review, !same n, !different n
      Answer the play history's tag questions
      !swapscan, !ytsync, !genrelists run
      Run a swap search, the YouTube Music sync, or the genre playlists now
